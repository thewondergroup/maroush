1981The first Maroush
opens on Edgware Road

London's first taste of Beirut

Marouf and Houda Abouzaki opened Maroush on Edgware Road in 1981 and helped make that street the home of Lebanese food in London. Forty-five years later it is still a family table, a late-night ritual and the place Londoners go for the real thing.
