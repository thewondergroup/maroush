Our story · since 1981

London’soriginalLebanese

Marouf and Houda Abouzaki opened the first Maroush on Edgware Road in 1981. Forty-five years later, we still cook the way they did: generously, traditionally and to share.

Est.1981Edgware Rd
How it started

From Beirut
to Edgware Road

Marouf and Houda came to London from Lebanon while war was tearing their country apart. Leaving family and friends behind was hard, but London made them welcome, and Londoners were just beginning to discover food from around the world.

In 1981 they opened Maroush on Edgware Road, serving Lebanese food exactly as it is made at home. It helped turn the street into London’s home of Lebanese cooking, and it is busier today than it was then.

The Lebanese table

Many small dishes,
shared slowly

Generosity

A Lebanese meal is never three courses. It is a table covered in small dishes, made to nourish and to entertain.

Sharing

Food is how we gather: family dinners, catching up with friends, closing a deal. Everything goes in the middle.

Hospitality

Conversation, family and a cup of kahwah. We want every guest to feel they have stepped into a home in Beirut.
