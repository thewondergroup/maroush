The room

Lively, late
and generous

A favourite with a mixed Knightsbridge crowd for decades. Drop in for a quick wrap to take away, share mezze at the counter, or settle into the first-floor dining room for a proper Lebanese feast.

  • First-floor dining room for fuller meals
  • Plenty of vegetarian and gluten-free dishes
  • Wraps to go, all evening
What to order

Start cold,
finish on the grill

Share a spread of mezze for the table, then move on to charcoal-grilled meats and shawarma from the spit.
